QUESTIONS

Before you book

Do I need to prepare anything? +
For Skill and Social Circles — nothing. Just show up. For Network Circles, prepare a 30-second intro.
What if my English is not strong? +
Skill Circles are for all levels. No grammar policing. Imperfect English is celebrated.
I'm introverted. Will I be forced to speak? +
You'll be gently invited, not forced. Most introverts speak more in one session than they do in a week.
What is the refund policy? +
Full refund if cancelled 12+ hours before. If we cancel, full refund plus a discount on your next booking.
How do I pay? +
We accept UPI payments via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app. Pay to speakcircle@slc, then upload your transaction ID to confirm your spot.
